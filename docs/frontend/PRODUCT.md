# SevenUp Frontend Product Context

## Product

Landing page for Clínica SevenUp, a clinic positioned around advanced aesthetic care, skin health, regenerative aesthetics, nutrition, pharmaceutical aesthetic care, and personalized evaluation in Santo Antônio da Platina, PR.

The current frontend is a static one-page HTML/CSS site. The code and visual assets are the source of truth.

## Audience

- People considering aesthetic facial/body treatments.
- People looking for natural-looking rejuvenation and clinically safe care.
- Local/regional visitors who need location, trust signals, treatments, and a clear appointment path.
- Specific commercial personas beyond this are TBD.

## Primary Goal

Convert qualified visitors into appointment requests through the "Agende Sua Avaliação Personalizada" form.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Understand what SevenUp offers and whether the tone feels premium, safe, and human.
- See available treatments and decide whether one matches their need.
- Trust the clinic through brand, real imagery, Dr. Sílvia context, testimonials, location, and FAQ.
- Find address/hours and route information.
- Request an evaluation with minimal friction.

## Main Flows

1. Hero impression: brand, visual mood, and promise.
2. Treatment discovery: Toxina Botulínica, Preenchimento, Bioestimuladores, Protocolos de Pele, Estética Corporal, Estética In & Out.
3. Clinic trust: physical space gallery.
4. About/authority: clinic story and Dra. Sílvia Letícia.
5. Social proof: patient testimonials.
6. Location: map, address, hours, route.
7. Appointment form.
8. FAQ.

## Interface Tone

Elegant, warm, clinical, restrained, organic, and editorial. The interface should feel high-care and local-premium, not SaaS-like, neon, generic wellness-template, or overly decorative.

## Appropriate Density

Moderate editorial density. Large display typography and image-led sections can breathe, but content should not become vague, empty, or ornamental. Mobile needs clear single-column progression and strong tap targets.

## UX Priorities

- Make the appointment path obvious without turning the page into aggressive sales UI.
- Preserve trust, safety, and naturalness as core messages.
- Use real clinic and treatment imagery as primary visual proof.
- Keep text readable on mobile and avoid overlap.
- Maintain section rhythm and consistent brand colors.
- Keep interactions simple: anchors, form, FAQ details, map route.
